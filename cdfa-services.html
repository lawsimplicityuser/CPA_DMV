Certified Divorce Financial Analyst support

Specialized CDFA analysis for high-stakes financial decisions.

Our Certified Divorce Financial Analyst (CDFA) services help divorcing spouses, high-net-worth individuals, attorneys, and mediators evaluate assets, liabilities, cash flow, support assumptions, tax impacts, retirement accounts, settlement scenarios, and long-term financial outcomes.

We provide CPA-grounded financial insight designed to clarify tradeoffs, support productive negotiations, and help clients make confident decisions without positioning CPA-DMV as a law firm.
