How we help

Control-focused advisory

Stronger processes reduce confusion and protect trust.

Detection

Financial irregularity review

Review unusual transactions, account activity, reconciliations, documentation gaps, and financial reporting concerns.

Controls

Internal control reviews

Evaluate approval workflows, segregation of duties, access controls, cash handling, vendor processes, and reporting oversight.

Reporting

Documentation support

Organize observations, supporting records, risk areas, and recommendations for leadership, boards, or professional advisors.

Common triggers

  • Unexpected cash, bank, or credit card activity.
  • Missing invoices, duplicate payments, or vendor concerns.
  • Weak controls over approval, access, or reconciliation processes.
  • Board or leadership requests for independent financial review.

Practical outcomes

  • Clearer understanding of risk areas and documentation gaps.
  • Better internal controls and approval workflows.
  • Organized support for leadership review and next steps.
  • Recommendations designed for implementation by real teams.
