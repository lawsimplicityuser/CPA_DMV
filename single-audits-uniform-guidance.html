Single Audits / Uniform Guidance

Organized audit support for organizations receiving federal awards.

Organizations that receive and administer federal funding must meet detailed compliance requirements, maintain accurate documentation, and demonstrate that funds are used properly. CPA-DMV specializes in Single Audits under Uniform Guidance for mission-driven and public-serving organizations.

How we help

Thorough, organized, and mission-aware Single Audit services.

Federal funding often supports essential programs. We help you maintain compliance, improve documentation, and build confidence among grantors, boards, and stakeholders.

  • Planning and performance of Single Audits under Uniform Guidance.
  • Review of federal award expenditures and compliance requirements.
  • Testing of internal controls over compliance.
  • Support with schedules of expenditures of federal awards, commonly called the SEFA.
  • Clear communication of findings, questioned costs, and corrective action needs.

Benefits

  • Improved audit readiness and cleaner federal award documentation.
  • Better understanding of compliance requirements before testing begins.
  • Clear reporting for boards, grantors, and stakeholders.
  • Practical guidance for corrective action plans and process improvements.

Who this service is for

  • Non-profit organizations receiving federal funding.
  • Governmental entities and program administrators.
  • Organizations preparing a SEFA for audit purposes.
  • Leadership teams that need a Single Audit Uniform Guidance CPA in the DMV area.
